The short version

Problem, work, answer

The whole project in three cards.

01 / problem

Too many unsold units

320 homes went on sale in April 2024. By the end of 2025, 144 had sold and 176 were still on the market, with handover starting in August 2026.

02 / work

15 files into one answer

I combined 15 messy files from 7 departments, measured how far each unit's price was above the company's own pricing policy, trained a model, and built dashboards.

03 / answer

It is the price

Units priced at the policy price sold 74%. Units about 15% above it sold only 16%.

NOTEThis is real company work. To protect confidential information, names, identifiers and figures were anonymized with AI before publishing. The patterns behind the findings were kept.

Recommendations

What I told management

Five actions, each backed by a finding above.

Bring overpriced units back to the policy price, not a cut across every unit.

Give villas their own price and offer.

Push longer payment plans before cash discounts.

Move marketing budget from Meta to referrals, events and existing clients.

Assign every lead to a broker.

Process

How I did it

Python (pandas, scikit-learn), Power BI (DAX, Power Query), Streamlit, Excel, HTML/JavaScript.

step_01

Clean

Fixed 121 problems across 15 files, like 20 spellings of 6 property types and prices 10× too high. Every fix was logged.

step_02

Model the data

Built a star schema so Power BI and Python read the same clean numbers.

step_03

Find the driver

Rebuilt the pricing policy for every unit and measured how far the real price was above it.

step_04

Predict & share

Logistic regression, 0.70 accuracy vs 0.55 baseline, using only facts known on launch day.
